This editable class plan is designed for beginners who want guided practice rather than a feature-by-feature software tour. Registration is not open yet; use the contact link to register your interest.

## Days 1–3: Foundations

Learn the workspace, document setup, layers, selections, exporting, and a dependable file workflow.

## Days 4–7: Image correction

Practise tonal and colour correction, retouching, masks, adjustment layers, and non-destructive editing.

## Days 8–11: Type and social graphics

Combine typography, shapes, images, and layout principles to build clear social-media graphics.

## Days 12–15: Posters and promotional design

Plan visual hierarchy and create posters, advertisements, and campaign variations for different formats.

## Days 16–18: Brand assets and workflows

Create reusable brand elements and practise an organised workflow from brief to export.

## Days 19–20: Final project

Complete, present, and refine a final project suitable for a beginner portfolio.
